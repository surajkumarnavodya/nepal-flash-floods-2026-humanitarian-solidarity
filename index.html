The Story

When the water rises, everyday life can change in an instant.

Severe flash flooding has swept through parts of northern Nepal, bringing sudden disruption to homes and communities. The floodwaters arrived while ordinary life was continuing — with children on their way to school or beginning their classes, families going about their day, and communities facing rapidly changing conditions.

This website is a quiet expression of solidarity: a place to understand the human context, stay thoughtful about information, and remember the people and communities navigating uncertainty.

Human impact
“To every family facing uncertainty, every person waiting for loved ones, and every community helping its neighbors — you are not alone.”

Behind every flood event are ordinary lives: homes, routines, relationships, livelihoods and plans temporarily placed on uncertain ground.

This space is not here to amplify fear. It is here to recognize people, honor those offering help, and keep compassion at the center of how we respond.

In difficult conditions, community support can take many forms: helping neighbors reach safer places, sharing reliable information, organizing essential supplies, and staying connected with people who may need assistance.

Information

Stay informed.
Stay thoughtful.

Flooding during the monsoon can disrupt homes, roads, local services and everyday life across vulnerable communities. The most important response is to keep people safe, follow local guidance, and share information carefully.

Monsoon and floodingHeavy seasonal rainfall can cause rivers and streams to rise, water to collect quickly, and transport routes to become difficult or unsafe. Local conditions can vary significantly from one community to another.
Life beyond the headlinesFlooding can affect homes, farms, roads, bridges, schools, businesses and access to everyday services. The consequences may continue after visible floodwater begins to recede.
Community responseNeighbors, local groups, volunteers and responders can play an important role in sharing information, assisting people, organizing essential supplies and supporting safer movement.
What to do with informationEmergency conditions can change quickly. Check the date and origin of a report, prefer official or established sources, and avoid forwarding information that cannot be confirmed.
Responsible supportDo not pressure people to donate or share personal information. If you choose to support relief work, verify the organization, request and payment details independently before acting.
Recovery is a processReturning to normal can take time. Recovery can involve restoring homes and livelihoods, repairing community infrastructure, reconnecting services and supporting people through the longer-term effects of disruption.
